Our Story

Saffron House began in 2012 with one family recipe book and a small kitchen on Park Street. The first dish we ever served was our grandmother's Kolkata biryani — and it is still the first thing most guests order.

Twelve years later the dining room is bigger, but the cooking has not changed: whole spices ground fresh every morning, biryani sealed on dum, and every plate finished by hand.

We cook the way we eat at home — slowly, generously, and for the people we love.

From our kitchen

Our kitchen team starts before sunrise — marinating, grinding spices, and preparing the day's gravies from scratch. Nothing comes from a packet, and nothing is rushed.

What you taste on the plate is what we tasted growing up: the same recipes, the same patience, the same love for feeding a full house.
